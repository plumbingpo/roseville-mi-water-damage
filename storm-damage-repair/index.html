Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes

Why Professional Storm Damage Repair Matters

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. An improperly installed tarp can create new problems by channeling water or failing under wind load. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
